Privacy

What the maker of Listen receives about you, which is as close to nothing as software can get. Last updated 27 August 2026.

The product

Listen runs entirely on your devices. Jacaranda Labs operates no server for it and requires no account. Your recordings, transcripts, notes and voiceprints are yours, on hardware you control, and we cannot see them. Nothing about you is collected beyond the anonymous statistics described below, which are on by default, yours to turn off any time, and never contain your content.

If you enable iCloud sync, data moves between your own devices through your own Apple account, sealed with a key we never hold. If you point the Ask feature at a hosted AI provider, the transcripts you ask about go to that provider under your agreement with them, not ours. The built-in OpenRouter connection requires a Zero Data Retention endpoint and denies provider data collection on every request; another endpoint follows that endpoint's own policy. Both are off until you turn them on.

Usage statistics and crash reports, on by default

Both apps send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports by default, and Settings, Privacy is where you turn it off. What can be sent is counts and durations in rough buckets, file sizes in rough buckets, which app a call was in from a fixed list, the kind of device holding a recording's audio (a Mac or an iPhone, never which one), stable failure codes, and crash stack traces. Never recordings, transcripts, notes, titles, names, tags, searches, questions, file paths, or raw error text. TELEMETRY.md is the complete dictionary of every event and property, and the filter that enforces it is public code both apps compile in.

  • Processor: PostHog Cloud, hosted in the EU (eu.i.posthog.com), configured to discard IP addresses at ingestion. Events are kept indefinitely: PostHog has no way to delete events after a set time, so nothing here can promise that it happens. What is deleted, immediately and on your instruction, is the install ID that connects them, which is the switch in Settings, Privacy.
  • Identity: a random per-install ID, never synced between your devices, deleted the moment you turn it off. Turning it back on later creates a fresh one.
  • Turning it off: the switch in Settings, Privacy. It stops the sending, deletes anything queued, and deletes the install ID. Organisations can force the whole feature off; see MANAGED.md.
  • Deletion requests: events are anonymous, so we cannot look up which are yours. Turning it off deletes the identifier that would connect future events; for anything beyond that, open an issue and we will work with PostHog's deletion tooling.

What we incidentally touch

Some infrastructure sees ordinary connection metadata, such as your IP address, because of how the internet works:

  • GitHub serves the update check (every two days), the downloads, and this website (GitHub Pages). GitHub's own privacy policy governs its logs. This website counts page views, and clicks on the download link, with a self-hosted, cookieless counter that stores no personal data and follows nobody across sites; there is no third-party analytics service on it. A download click records which of the page's download links was used, and nothing about you.
  • Hugging Face serves the one-time speech model download.
  • Apple handles App Store and TestFlight distribution of the iPhone app, under Apple's terms.

None of these connections carries your recordings, transcripts, or anything derived from them.

Your responsibilities as the recorder

You are the controller of what you record. Recording other people is regulated nearly everywhere: consent rules differ by country and by US state, and workplaces have their own. Listen gives you the tools (deletion that propagates everywhere, voiceprint forgetting, an activity log), and the obligation to record lawfully is yours.

Data rights

Every right a data subject might exercise against a Listen library is exercised on the device that holds it, by its owner: the library is open WAV and JSON files, so access and portability are the file system, and deletion is deletion, propagated to every synced device and to iCloud. There is no copy at Jacaranda Labs to ask about, correct, or erase.
