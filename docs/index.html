Listen

Remember what was said.

No bot joins your call. Your recordings stay on your Mac.

Download for macOS

For Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later).

This looks like an Intel Mac, and Listen will not run on it.

Listen transcribes on your own machine, and that needs the Neural Engine Apple introduced with the M1 in late 2020. There is no Intel version, and there is no version that would work.

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it says Chip rather than Processor, this guess is wrong and you can download Listen as normal.

A demo meeting. The people and voices are synthetic.

Stay in the meeting

Listen records both sides of the call. When you need something later, play it back and read along.

Keep your own notes

Write down what matters while you listen. When you want a summary, your assistant can make one from the meetings you choose.

A demo of personal notes and a summary across two meetings.

Your meetings stay on your Mac

There is no meeting bot and no account. Your audio, transcripts and voiceprints never leave your Mac.

Listen downloads its speech model once and checks for updates. It does not upload your meetings or collect telemetry.

Catch the start

Press Record when the meeting begins. Decide whether to keep it when you are done.

Speak before the meeting

For thoughts you want to get down before the call, there is Speak. It turns your voice into text in any Mac app.

Install with Homebrew

brew trust --cask mugoosse/tap/listen
brew install --cask mugoosse/tap/listen

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. System audio capture needs macOS 14.2; below that Listen records your microphone only.

The speech model is about 2.5 GB and downloads on first run. If you use Speak already, Listen uses the copy on your Mac.
