Listen

Turns your meetings into text and works out who said what.

Entirely on your Mac. No bot, no account, nothing uploaded.

Download for macOS

For Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later).

This looks like an Intel Mac, and Listen will not run on it.

Listen transcribes on your own machine, and that needs the Neural Engine Apple introduced with the M1 in late 2020. There is no Intel version, and there is no version that would work.

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it says Chip rather than Processor, this guess is wrong and you can download Listen as normal.

What it does

You press record. Nobody joins the call, nothing appears on the calendar, and no one else is told the meeting is being recorded by a service, because there is no service.

Both sides of the call

Your microphone and everything your Mac is playing, on two separate tracks. It uses a Core Audio process tap, which asks for audio recording permission and not screen recording. Hearing a meeting should not cost access to everything on your screen.

Fast enough not to think about

Parakeet through MLX, about 240 times faster than real time on an M4 Max. An hour-long meeting is written up in well under a minute, and transcription starts on its own as soon as you keep a recording.

Who said what

Speakers are separated on the Neural Engine. Your own track needs no guessing at all: the microphone is you by definition, which removes the single most common mistake this kind of tool makes.

Voices it has heard before

Name someone once and Listen suggests them the next time it hears them. Suggestions are ranked and never applied on their own, so the transcript never quietly claims somebody said something.

Ask an agent about your own meetings

Listen ships an MCP server. Read-only, no port, and the app does not need to be running.

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "listen": {
      "command": "/usr/local/bin/listen",
      "args": ["mcp"]
    }
  }
}

Settings has this ready to copy with the right path already filled in.

What leaves your Mac

huggingface.coOnce, to download the speech model.
github.comEvery two days, to check for an update.

That is the whole list, and it is declared in the app's InternetAccessPolicy.plist so a firewall like Little Snitch can hold it to that. Audio, transcripts and voiceprints are never uploaded, and there is no telemetry.

Recording first, deciding after

Recording begins the moment you press Start and writes to disk immediately. Listen asks whether to keep it when you stop, not before.

That order is deliberate. A recorder that waits for you to confirm has already lost the first minute, which is the part where everybody says who they are. Walk away without answering and the audio is deleted after 24 hours.

Its companion

For the thought you want to get down before the meeting, there is Speak. It turns your voice into text in any Mac app, privately and without an account.

Other ways to install

Prefer Homebrew?

brew install --cask mugoosse/tap/listen

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. System audio capture needs macOS 14.2; below that Listen records your microphone only.

The speech model is about 2.5 GB and downloads on first run, after you press the button that says so. If you already use Speak it is already on your disk, and Listen will find it rather than fetching it twice.
