# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need a private, local macOS meeting recorder and transcript library. They begin and monitor capture from the menu bar, often while their attention is on a call rather than the app itself.

## Product Purpose

Listen records meetings locally, transcribes them, and helps identify speakers without a bot, account, or upload. Its status glyph must communicate listening and recording in a crowded macOS menu bar.

## Brand Personality

Calm, attentive, crafted. Listen is blue, and its mascot is a friendly monkey whose hands sit behind its ears.

## Anti-references

Generic waveform or microphone symbols; overly detailed miniature mascots; decorative status-bar artwork that loses clarity at 16 points.

## Design Principles

- Keep the full character in the Dock and product storytelling, not the status bar.
- Use the Good Pair's shared square seal in the menu bar: 聞 (hear) for Listen.
- Preserve macOS template-icon restraint while retaining a recognisable family cue.
- Let recording state add information without changing the icon vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use monochrome template imagery that adapts to macOS light and dark menu bars. Do not communicate status by colour alone; maintain a distinct recording silhouette and descriptive tooltips.
