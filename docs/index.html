Remember the meeting, not the recorder.

Listen keeps a private, searchable memory of who said what, what you noticed, and what needs to happen next.

Entirely on your Mac. No bot, no account, nothing uploaded.

Download for macOS

For Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later).

This looks like an Intel Mac, and Listen will not run on it.

Listen transcribes on your own machine, and that needs the Neural Engine Apple introduced with the M1 in late 2020. There is no Intel version, and there is no version that would work.

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it says Chip rather than Processor, this guess is wrong and you can download Listen as normal.

A real playback capture from an invented meeting. The people, conversation and synthesised voices are all fake.

The part you need, back in context

When someone says “what did Priya mean by that?”, you can replay the exact moment, see the words as they are spoken, and know who said them.

Both sides, one conversation

Listen captures your microphone and what your Mac is playing as separate tracks. A call is not a monologue, and a meeting record should not be either.

Know who said it

Your own voice never has to be guessed: the microphone is you. Name the other people once and Listen suggests them when it hears them again, without silently changing a transcript behind your back.

Find the meeting you remember

Meetings take the calendar name already on your Mac. Browse by recording, person or note instead of trying to remember a filename, then search inside the part of the library you are looking at.

Ready before the next meeting starts

Transcription begins as soon as you keep a recording. On an M4 Max, an hour-long meeting is written up in well under a minute.

Keep the conclusion with the evidence

The useful thing from a meeting is often the thought that never made it into the transcript.

Every recording has a notes page that is yours. Open it and there is a cursor: no button to press first, no template to choose, nothing saved until you type. It works while the call is still running, which is when it is worth the most. "We should upsell them" is in no transcript and cannot be reconstructed from one.

When you want a write-up, ask your preferred assistant for the summary, decisions or open questions. It comes back as a separate note linked to every meeting it used, so a conclusion from four catch-ups belongs to all four.

Your own notes are read-only to it. It reads what you wrote, and writes beside it. It cannot rename anybody, edit a transcript, or delete a recording.

Settings has the configuration ready to copy. There is no port, no account, and the app does not even need to be running.

A synthetic library: invented meetings, people and text.

Private means no one else joins

There is no meeting bot, account or upload in the middle. Listen records and transcribes on this Mac, and the library stays here.

huggingface.coOnce, to download the speech model.
github.comEvery two days, to check for an update.

That is the whole list. Audio, transcripts and voiceprints are never uploaded, and there is no telemetry.

Never lose the first minute

Recording begins the moment you press Start and writes to disk immediately. Listen asks whether to keep it when you stop, not before.

That order is deliberate. A recorder that waits for you to confirm has already lost the first minute, which is the part where everybody says who they are. Walk away without answering and the audio is deleted after 24 hours.

For the thought before the meeting

For the thought you want to get down before the meeting, there is Speak. It turns your voice into text in any Mac app, privately and without an account.

Other ways to install

Prefer Homebrew?

brew trust --cask mugoosse/tap/listen
brew install --cask mugoosse/tap/listen

The first line is Homebrew 6.0 and later refusing to load a cask from a tap that is not one of its own until you say so. Install without it and Homebrew stops and tells you the same thing.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. System audio capture needs macOS 14.2; below that Listen records your microphone only.

The speech model is about 2.5 GB and downloads on first run, after you press the button that says so. If you already use Speak it is already on your disk, and Listen will find it rather than fetching it twice.
